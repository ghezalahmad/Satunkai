Privacy Policy for Satūnkai

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Introduction

Satūnkai ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Video URLs: When you paste a URL to download a video, we process this URL to extract the video content. We do not store these URLs on our servers.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and app improvement purposes.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: We display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo/React Native: Our app is built using Expo and React Native frameworks, which may collect crash reports and analytics data.

Data Storage

  • Downloaded videos are stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not upload your downloaded content to any external servers.
  • App preferences are stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymous, aggregated data for analytics purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete downloaded content from your device at any time
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
